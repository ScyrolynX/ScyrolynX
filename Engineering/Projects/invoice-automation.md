# Automated Invoice & Reporting System

## Problem

Small businesses often spend time manually creating invoices
and preparing reports from their sales data.

## Proposed Solution

Build an automated system that takes structured sales data,
processes it, generates professional invoices and reports,
and stores the resulting records.

## Target Customers

- E-commerce businesses
- Small businesses
- Bookkeeping/accounting firms

## Initial Goal

Build a working prototype that demonstrates the complete
automation workflow from input data to final invoice/report.
